## Platform

web

## Users
Three audiences with equal weight on the home page (confirmed 2026-11-12):
- **Cataract surgeons and clinics**: choose which IOL to implant and order it (by model number, diopter and PO).
- **Distributors / importers**: buy by volume per country, register products locally, may sell under private label.
- **Patients facing cataract surgery**: want plain-language understanding of cataract, surgery and lens types.

The page is also a sales proposal: EyeKon's owners will judge it as "what our site could be".

## Product Purpose
Website for EyeKon Medical, Inc., a privately owned US manufacturer of intraocular lenses (PMMA, hydrophilic acrylic, hydrophobic, preloaded system), capsular tension rings, viscoelastics and eye wipes. It replaces a dated catalog-style site (carousel without message, English only, blog stalled in 2024). Success: each audience finds its path within seconds (products/order, distribution/private label, patient information), and the vision simulator reads as EyeKon's differentiator against Rayner-class competitors.

## Positioning
- Lenses are lathe-cut (not molded) and made in their own 17,000 ft² plant in Clearwater, Florida; free of vacuoles/glistening.
- Natural Yellow series uses 3-hydroxykynurenine, the chromophore of the young crystalline lens.
- 30+ years; first FDA approval 1989 (one page says 1985: unconfirmed); CE since 1996; hydrophilic line certified under EU MDR 2017/745 (2026); ISO 13485:2016; 100+ countries; UK affiliate EyeKon Medical, Ltd.; private label for 8 brands.
- **IOLSIMULATOR** (our product): VR on Meta Quest 3 + doctor's tablet, kit in a carry case; reproduces per eye how a patient sees with each EyeKon lens. Already configured with EyeKon's own catalog (Monofocal, Edof Serie 2, Multifocal) and EyeKon-skinned scenes (Miami beach office, reading book with EyeKon logo, living room cards, night road with EyeKon sign). Strong but secondary on the home page; own dedicated page.

## Operating Context
Surgeons order by model number + diopter + PO; prices FOB Clearwater; IFUs and brand catalogs downloaded as PDFs; customer service in English and Spanish, Mon–Fri 8–5 EST. Many products are "not for sale in the US" (CTR, viscoelastic, hydrophilic, hydrophobic, anterior chamber). Federal law Rx-only notice applies.

## Product Principles
- Show, don't claim: the lens's effect on vision is demonstrable, so demonstrate it.
- One site, three doors: surgeons, distributors and patients each get a clear path from the first screen.
- Manufacturing truth over marketing gloss: lathe-cut, Florida-made, certified — stated precisely.
- Plain language for patients; exact specs for professionals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older audience (patients 60+, many with cataract): text must be zoomable, base size generous, high contrast, no meaning carried by color alone.
