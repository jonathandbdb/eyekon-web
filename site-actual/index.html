Intraocular Lens Manufacturer

We are an FDA approved Intraocular Lens Manufacturer (IOL), that also manufactures Capsular Tension Rings ( CTR ) and Viscoelastic (Visco). EyeKon Medical, Inc. is a privately owned 17,000 square feet manufacturing facility located in sunny Clearwater, Florida.

EyeKon Medical has been responsible for restoring the quality of life for over 30 years. Our intraocular lenses, and all our products are manufactured right here in the United States.

As a leading intraocular lens manufacturer, we are dedicated to the design, development and manufacture of ophthalmic surgical devices using only the finest materials, workmanship and quality control practices with products currently marketed in the United States and in over 100 countries worldwide. EyeKon is an FDA approved facility. We received our first US FDA approval with the PMMA Posterior Intraocular lens in 1989.

New · Virtual reality for your practice

Let your patients see how they will see — before surgery

IOLSIMULATOR is a virtual-reality vision simulator loaded with the EyeKon lens catalog. The patient puts on the headset and experiences real-life scenes through each EyeKon IOL, while the surgeon guides the session from a tablet.

What comes in the kit

Everything arrives configured and ready to use in the consultation room — and fits in a single carry case, so it travels easily from clinic to clinic.

  • 1Meta Quest 3 VR headsetWith both controllers. The patient wears it during the simulation.
  • 2Android tablet — physician consoleIOLSIMULATOR app installed: pick the EyeKon lens, the scene and the eye.
  • 3Hard-shell carry caseCustom foam inserts to store and transport the whole set.

Your patient puts it on — and sees the difference

Comparing lenses stops being an abstract explanation. The patient lives it.

  1. The patient puts on the Meta Quest 3 headset.
  2. The surgeon selects EyeKon lenses on the tablet: monofocal, EDOF, multifocal…
  3. The patient compares near, intermediate and night-driving vision in real-life scenes.
