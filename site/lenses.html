Hydrophilic acrylic IOLs

A proprietary copolymer of hydrophilic and hydrophobic monomers that behaves like the natural crystalline lens — excellent folding and biocompatibility. Every lens is screened with our Clear View process for unpolymerized material.

  • Free of vacuoles and glistening; does not stick to instruments
  • Square-edge design to reduce posterior capsule opacification
  • Co-aspheric optics across the whole range
  • Monofocal, EDOF and accommodative series

Natural Yellow (NY) versions filter UVA and violet light using 3-hydroxykynurenine, the same chromophore found in the young human lens.

Hydrophobic IOLs

Lathe-cut — not molded — hydrophobic acrylic with a high refractive index, for a thinner lens, smaller incision and lower risk of PCO, inflammation and bacterial adhesion.

  • Free of vacuoles and glistening thanks to lathe cutting
  • Aspheric optic that offsets the eye's positive spherical aberration
  • High tensile strength, controlled unfolding

PMMA IOLs

Time-tested PMMA-CQ UV, the material used since the very first intraocular lens in 1949. Lathe-cut from buttons or rods in one-piece and three-piece designs.

  • FDA-approved single-piece posterior chamber lenses
  • Excellent tissue tolerance and long-term stability
  • The most cost-effective option for high-volume programs
