Show patients what they will see — before surgery.

A virtual reality simulator that reproduces, eye by eye and in stereo, how a patient would see with each intraocular lens. The surgeon controls everything from a tablet.

Three steps, one consultation

The patient puts on the headset

A light Meta Quest 3 headset — no cables, no setup. Each eye receives its own simulation.

The surgeon picks the lenses

From the tablet, choose any lens in the EyeKon catalog for each eye and switch scenes in real time.

Patient and surgeon decide together

Compare two lenses side by side — reading, intermediate distance and night driving with halos and glare.

Compare EyeKon lenses, scene by scene

Pick a scene and the two lenses to compare, then drag the slider. In the headset, the patient sees this in 3D all around them.

Simulation for educational purposes. It is not a medical device and does not replace a clinical evaluation.

Everything in one case, ready in minutes

  • Meta Quest 3 headset with the simulator installed
  • Android tablet with the doctor's control app
  • Protective carry case for clinics and trade shows
  • Configured with the EyeKon lens catalog — new lenses added remotely
  • Works on any local Wi-Fi network; no internet needed during the session
