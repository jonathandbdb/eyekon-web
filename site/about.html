About EyeKon Medical

A privately owned medical device manufacturer of intraocular lenses, capsular tension rings and viscoelastics.

Who we are

EyeKon Medical operates a 17,000 square foot manufacturing facility in Clearwater, Florida. All our products are made in the United States.

For more than three decades we have supplied surgeons and distributors in over 100 countries, with a registered affiliate in the United Kingdom.

Our history

From PMMA to hydrophilic EDOF lenses: decades of continuous development.

  1. 1949Sir Harold Ridley implants the first PMMA intraocular lens — the material we still lathe today.
  2. 1989First FDA approval for an EyeKon posterior chamber PMMA lens.
  3. 1996First CE-marked EyeKon lenses for Europe.
  4. 2020sHydrophilic, hydrophobic, EDOF and preloaded systems join the portfolio.
  5. 2026Hydrophilic acrylic portfolio certified under EU MDR 2017/745.
  6. TodayIOLSIMULATOR: patients preview EyeKon lenses in virtual reality.
